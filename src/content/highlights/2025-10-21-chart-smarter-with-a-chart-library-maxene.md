The Justice Innovation Lab guide includes a detailed chart-type section that starts with orientation guidance and then moves into concrete examples.

For each chart category, the guide shows styled variations and explains when to use each pattern. This gives readers practical options rather than only a do-this-not-that framing.

When chart libraries pair examples with decision guidance, teams can move faster without sacrificing consistency.
