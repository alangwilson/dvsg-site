Many style guides provide little detail on interactive and animated charts, likely because implementation options vary widely.

The UK Office for National Statistics guide is a strong exception. It outlines interaction patterns (hover, scrolling, inputs), explains when interactivity is useful, and includes practical implementation considerations.

It also addresses tradeoffs directly: interactions can hide key insights or increase user effort, so interactivity should be used intentionally rather than by default.
