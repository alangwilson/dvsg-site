Symbol guidance in data visualization is more than choosing shapes. It affects interpretation, uncertainty cues, and legend clarity.

The WHO design system uses side-by-side examples and practical recommendations that make symbol usage clear and repeatable.

Instead of listing symbols in isolation, it frames symbol choices as solutions to communication problems, which improves consistency and comprehension.
