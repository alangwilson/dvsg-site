The Justice Innovation Lab style guide includes a comprehensive chart-type chapter that helps readers choose charts that fit their story.

Within each chart type, the guide provides multiple stylistic examples and practical notes on usage. Rather than only flagging mistakes, it shows usable alternatives that support flexibility.

This combination of examples plus guidance makes chart libraries much more actionable for cross-functional teams.
