A low-effort way to improve accessibility guidance is to annotate color swatches with recommended foreground text colors.

The Justice Innovation Lab guide demonstrates this pattern by indicating when black or white text should be used on each swatch. This helps designers make faster, safer choices during implementation.

It is still important to verify contrast thresholds for each swatch/background pairing against WCAG targets.
