Examples make style guides tangible, and contrastive examples are especially effective.

When teams show both recommended and non-recommended chart treatments, readers can compare outcomes directly and understand the rationale behind the guidance.

This pattern gives users multiple ways to learn: written rule, strong example, and cautionary example.
