Linking directly to implementation templates can make a style guide much easier to use.

Kraft Heinz places template links next to chart guidance and supports multiple tools, including Figma, Tableau, and PowerBI.

This keeps guidance and execution resources in one place and improves adoption, even when some templates remain private behind sign-in controls.
