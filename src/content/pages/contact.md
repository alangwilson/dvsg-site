A data visualization style guide is a practical playbook for how your team designs charts, tables, and dashboards. It turns broad brand standards into clear decisions for data work, including color use, typography, labeling, annotation, chart selection, and accessibility.

Style guides matter because they help teams communicate with more clarity and less friction. Instead of reinventing decisions project by project, people can use shared standards that improve quality, consistency, and trust.

The resources on this site are designed to help you build that playbook faster:

- Tips show focused lessons from real style guides so you can apply proven ideas right away.
- Examples let you study how organizations document visualization rules in practice.
- Resources provide deeper guidance on process, systems, and communication so your guide is useful in day-to-day work.

For collaboration, speaking, or content suggestions, send a note through your preferred channel.

When sharing chart examples for review, include:

- The question your audience needs answered
- The data source and date range
- The chart type you considered and why
